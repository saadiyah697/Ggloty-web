Part I: How We Handle Your Data

1. Who We Are and Scope

1.1. "Ggloty", "we", "us" and "our" refer to [LEGAL ENTITY NAME], with its registered office at [REGISTERED ADDRESS]. We operate the Ggloty website and mobile apps (the "Platform").

1.2. This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, who receives it, how long we keep it, and your choices.

1.3. For privacy questions or complaints, contact our Privacy Officer using Section 12.

2. Data We Collect and Why

The following table summarizes the data we collect, why we use it, and who receives it:

  • Account & Contact Data: Name, phone, email, profile photo — used to run your account and bookings.
  • Booking Data: History, service address, appointment details — used to complete bookings.
  • Uploaded Content: Photos, videos, portfolios — used to display your profile.
  • Verification Data: Identity documents, GST details — used to verify Service Providers.
  • Payment Data: Transaction amounts, payment status — full card details handled only by Razorpay.
  • Device Data: OS, app version, crash logs — used to improve platform stability.
  • Communications: Support messages, complaints — used to resolve issues.

3. How We Use Your Data

We use your data to create accounts, facilitate bookings, verify users, process payments, provide support, improve the Platform, detect fraud, and comply with legal obligations.

4. Sharing and Third Parties

We share data only with:

  • The other party to a booking
  • Service providers acting for us (Razorpay, hosting, messaging, analytics)
  • Authorities where required by law

We do not sell your data or share it with third‑party advertising or AI services.

5. Device Permissions

The app may request access to camera, photos, location, and notifications — all optional and used only for relevant features.
